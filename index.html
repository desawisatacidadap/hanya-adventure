What is Outbound? Meaning, Benefits & Outbound in Puncak Bogor

Outbound adalah kegiatan pelatihan berbasis permainan dan tantangan fisik di alam terbuka yang bertujuan membangun kerja sama tim, komunikasi, dan kepemimpinan. Outbound artinya membawa peserta keluar dari rutinitas kantor menuju lingkungan alam seperti hutan pinus dan pegunungan. Hanya Adventure Puncak Bogor menghadirkan program outbound korporat, sekolah, dan komunitas sejak 2012, dipadukan dengan aktivitas ATV, paintball, dan jeep offroad.

Outbound is a training activity based on games and physical challenges in the outdoors, aimed at building teamwork, communication, and leadership. Hanya Adventure Puncak Bogor has delivered corporate, school, and community outbound programs since 2012, combined with ATV, paintball, and jeep offroad activities.
